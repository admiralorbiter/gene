## 2. Reinterpret: What Recent Results Imply
The central lesson of Stage 8C-R1 is that **Existence and Identity are distinct epistemic categories**:
- *Existence* requires low evidentiary friction: explicit deployment/commissioning notices justify reversible provisional creation.
- *Identity* requires high evidentiary friction: exact registered matching or explicit parenthetical corroboration is required to authorize canonical linkage.

By separating these two authorities, GENE eliminates the neural proposal coverage bottleneck without relaxing canonical false-merge safety.
