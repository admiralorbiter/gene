# Fact Check & Guardrails — Podcast V2

## GENE claims that are currently safe if scoped correctly

### Experiment 0

Safe:

> GENE's calibrated Experiment 0 distinguishes exposure, model-reported support, and counterfactually tested causal influence in controlled synthetic worlds.

Safe:

> Information ecology and response-contract design materially changed intervention performance in the tested Gemma 3 12B calibration assay.

Do not say:

> Gemma is 100% causally faithful.

The 100% figure belongs only to one small hardened calibration cell.

---

### Experiment 1A

Safe:

> In the tested controlled branching assay, a false founder premise propagated through locally valid deductions while preserving ancestral semantic identity across transformations.

Prefer:

> “lossless semantic transmission in the tested symbolic channel”

rather than:

> “perfect misinformation transmission in LLMs generally.”

Do not imply the observed reproduction number of two was surprising independent of the designed branching topology.

---

### Retrieval / B1c

Safe:

> In the calibrated matched assay, complete support configurations enabled expression for both healthy and infected lineages, while broken paths produced abstention.

Safe:

> Retrieval competence was content-agnostic in the tested assay.

Do not generalize this to arbitrary RAG systems or real-world evidence.

---

### Selective immunity / C1b

Safe:

> In the balanced shared synthetic ecology, lineage-blind controls showed null healthy-versus-infected selectivity, while ancestry-aware quarantine preserved the target of an externally supplied risk judgment through descendant relationships.

Critical boundary:

> **Genealogy does not detect truth.**

It allows a detector judgment to remain attached to the relevant family.

Do not call TPR−FPR a universal “immunity law.” It is an identity of the frozen analytical policy/topology.

---

### Node-only laundering

Safe:

> Root-only quarantine provided no containment in the tested descendant-mediated topology because future tasks consumed surviving descendants rather than the removed root.

Do not generalize to all memory architectures.

---

### C2/C2a

Safe:

> In a small live Gemma 3 12B panel, lineage-level retrieval interventions translated into the predicted behavioral differences.

Safe:

> The local execution stack produced different outcomes under repeated bit-for-bit request-equivalent calls despite temperature 0 and fixed seed.

Say:

> “empirical replay non-determinism/instability on this execution stack.”

Do not claim a specific GPU/kernel mechanism unless separately measured.

---

### C2b pseudo-path findings

Safe:

> In the tested 30-call panel, explicit mismatched-route evidence elicited clean abstention in all observed mismatch calls, while no-route conditions elicited unsupported concrete outputs in all observed no-route calls.

Do not say:

> “the attention mechanism recognized the mismatch.”

Attention was not measured.

Do not say:

> “foreign target routes universally cause cross-binding.”

The foreign exact-match route produced errors in the swapped KESTREL role but abstention in the forward VELORA role.

Use:

> “role/entity-sensitive pseudo-path formation.”

---

### Structural proofreader

Safe:

> The Support-Certificate Validator checks whether the model-reported parent memories structurally instantiate the antecedents of the rule corresponding to the emitted conclusion.

Safe:

> In the tested C2b panel, it admitted all observed valid certificates and rejected all observed unsupported concrete outputs.

Critical boundary:

> **A valid reported support certificate is not evidence that the cited memories causally generated the output.**

GENE explicitly distinguishes reported support from causal support.

Do not call Layer 2 a causal proofreader.

---

### Expression vs heritability

Before audio generation, pull the final corrected values from the canonical results manifest / frozen report.

Do not reuse stale values from intermediate commits.

The conceptual distinction is safe:

```text
mu_expression = probability an unsupported candidate is emitted
mu_heritable = probability an unsupported candidate is admitted into persistent reproductive memory
```

The podcast should emphasize the distinction more than the exact percentage.

## Fresh outside-story guardrails

### Manuscript stemmatics

Safe:

> Textual critics have long reconstructed manuscript families using shared errors/innovations, often representing relationships with a stemma codicum.

Safe:

> Contamination — copying from multiple exemplars — complicates simple tree reconstruction.

Do not imply the method always uniquely recovers the true historical tree.

---

### Citation-copying story

Safe:

> Repeated identical citation misprints have been used as evidence that bibliographic references can be copied from other reference lists rather than independently reconstructed.

Do not state a dramatic percentage of scientists who “never read citations” as settled fact. The numerical inference is model-dependent and has been debated.

---

### Kinetic proofreading

Safe:

> Hopfield proposed an additional driven kinetic step that can increase biochemical recognition specificity beyond simple equilibrium discrimination.

Use as analogy:

> extra verification before commitment can improve selectivity.

Do not say GENE's validator is literally kinetic proofreading.

---

### Immune tolerance

Safe:

> Burnet and Medawar's work helped establish acquired immunological tolerance and the idea that effective immunity requires discrimination, not maximal reaction.

Use autoimmunity as a systems analogy only.

Do not map specific immune cell mechanisms onto GENE policies unless explicitly qualified.

---

### Continued influence effect

Safe:

> Misinformation can continue to influence reasoning after correction, although corrections generally can reduce misinformation's impact and effectiveness varies by context.

Do not say corrections “don't work.”

---

## Biological language policy

Biological terms are operational metaphors unless explicitly defined by the experiment.

Allowed when defined:

- locus
- allele
- mutation
- descendant
- lineage
- reproduction
- expression
- heritability
- extinction
- proofreading
- immunity / autoimmunity as architecture names

Avoid implying:

- consciousness;
- life;
- literal genes;
- literal infection;
- natural selection occurring inside a single model call.

A useful recurring disclaimer is:

> “The biology is useful here because it forces us to ask measurable questions, not because the memory system is literally an organism.”

---

## Statistics / denominator policy

When a host gives a percentage, also give or quickly imply the denominator when practical.

Prefer:

> “all six no-route calls in this small panel”

rather than:

> “Gemma always does this.”

Treat world/configuration/replay clustering seriously.

Repeated invocations of the same prompt are execution observations, not independent semantic worlds.

---

## Provenance and lineage integrity assumption

The current GENE immune experiments assume the ancestry graph maintained by the harness is correct.

This is a major scope boundary.

Real memory systems can:

- summarize;
- merge;
- lose metadata;
- copy through tools;
- manufacture apparent corroboration.

The podcast should explicitly say:

> **Lineage-aware governance only works as well as the lineage record it trusts.**

This naturally motivates future provenance-decay work.

---

## Final style rule

Whenever a sentence sounds exciting, ask:

> “Did GENE demonstrate that, or did the analogy suggest it?”

If the latter, signal it as analogy, interpretation, or future question.
